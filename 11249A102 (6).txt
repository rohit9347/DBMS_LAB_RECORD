SQL> -- HAVING Clause
SQL> SELECT DEPARTMENT, AVG(MARKS) AS AVG_MARKS
  2  FROM STUDENT
  3  GROUP BY DEPARTMENT
  4  HAVING AVG(MARKS) > 85;

-------------------- ----------
CSE                        86.5
IT                         93.5
